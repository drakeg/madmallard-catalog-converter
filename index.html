How to use this converter

  1. Click Try sample data to open two fictional product rows here, without downloading anything. Or choose your own text PDF or CSV.
  2. Match the extracted columns to SKU, description, price and unit; click Apply column mapping.
  3. Compare every SKU and price to the supplier source. Edit incorrect values, remove headers and check Verified? for confirmed rows. Editing resets verification.
  4. Download reviewed CSV for valid, verified rows. Download exceptions CSV for items needing correction or review; edit that CSV and upload it below to preview and apply fixes.
  5. Open reviewed-catalog.csv in Excel, LibreOffice Calc or Google Sheets. Match it to your inventory system's import format, then import manually. This app does not update inventory.

CSV is the output spreadsheet, not another input you must upload. The sample is fictional and should never be imported into real inventory.

Reusable supplier mapping profiles

Saving is optional. Store only the column positions and PDF-header choice in this browser (not your supplier files). Profiles are local to this device and browser, not shared across devices.

Applying a profile replaces the current column mapping and resets all row verification. Check extracted values before accepting exports.

What is in each downloaded file?

Reviewed CSV: only verified and valid products. Open in a spreadsheet editor, confirm the expected columns and import it into your inventory system manually.

Exceptions CSV: rows still unverified or with warnings, with source locations and reasons. Edit the product fields, save as CSV, then upload it using the correction form below. Do not change workspace_id, row_id or source.

If the reviewed download is disabled, verify at least one valid product row first.

3. Fix issues in a spreadsheet, then bring them back

Download Exceptions CSV above. Open needs-review.csv in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice, fix the sku, description, price, unit columns, and save as CSV. Keep workspace_id, row_id, and source unchanged; they match your edits to the right products. Leave the original catalog open in this browser tab.
